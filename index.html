👩‍🚀 About Me

I am Naiqui M. Armendariz, a Mechanical Engineer with a completed Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering and a strong background in aerospace systems, automation, and multidisciplinary engineering design. I am currently seeking full-time engineering opportunities where I can contribute hands-on technical expertise, systems thinking, and rapid problem-solving to high-impact projects.

My experience spans advanced prototyping, structural and thermal analysis, and real-world system integration—bridging engineering fundamentals with practical application across aerospace, sustainability, and research environments. I have worked on projects ranging from ISS-flown experiments and microgravity materials research to award-winning environmental engineering systems and rapid-prototyping using 3D printing technologies.

My technical skill set includes Finite Element Analysis (ANSYS), CAD/CAE (SolidWorks, CATIA), thermomechanical design, residual stress analysis, and mechanical mounting system integration. I also bring strong automation and programming experience using Python, along with hardware interfacing using Arduino and ESP32 microcontrollers, sensors, and data pipelines.

Professionally, I completed an internship with Supernal (Hyundai Motor Group’s eVTOL division), where I contributed to mechanical mounting systems and design integration for future air mobility platforms. In academic and research settings, I have supported FEA-driven design decisions, experimental validation, and cross-functional collaboration in fast-paced environments.

  • Designed and tested microgravity samples for the RISE Lab, including FEA-driven mechanical components and thermal stress analysis.
  • Co-developed a wind-powered water filtration system awarded the Terry McManus Outstanding Student Award at the 35th WERC Environmental Design Contest.
  • Developed an ISS-selected research payload through the Student Spaceflight Experiments Program (SSEP), earning 1st Place in Undergraduate Research at EPCC.

Beyond engineering, I have founded and led automation and technical outreach initiatives, building tools that streamline workflows, support research teams, and expand STEM access for underserved communities. I bring a combination of technical depth, practical execution, leadership, and adaptability—ready to contribute immediately in mechanical, aerospace, or advanced R&D roles.
